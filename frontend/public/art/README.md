# Midnight Archive artwork

Original generated artwork, created for Pensieve on 6 October 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. Both WebP files are locally bundled.

- **memory-chamber.webp** — 1672 × 941, approximately 177 KB. Loading poster and WebGL fallback. Its bowl is part of the illustration; the running WebGL scene renders its own interactive bowl, liquid, books, desk and instruments.
- **archive-architecture.webp** — 1672 × 941, approximately 253 KB. Distant architecture backplate in the live 3D scene. UV cropping uses the upper 72% so the foreground floor remains geometric.
- **memory-interior.webp** — approximately 241 KB, created on 7 October 2026. Distant submerged archive architecture during the descent. The foreground papers, gothic ribs, currents and camera are actual 3D. [Exact prompt and provenance](memory-interior-prompt.md).

The stone basin, liquid shader, rounded furniture, original inlay glyphs, book meshes, material maps and folio ink are generated by project code. There are no imported film models or franchise logos.

**Architecture generation prompt:** Cinematic photoreal PBR fantasy library interior, widescreen 16:9. Human eye-level view down a walnut library aisle facing an ornate tall arched observatory window. Aged tooled leather books at the margins, carved limestone pilasters, vaulted ribs, small brass candle sconces, amber interior light and a cool blue-grey moonlit night beyond the window. Readable dark wood and restrained stone wear. Lower 40% empty matte stone floor for separate live foreground props. No desk, bowl, pedestal, cauldron, liquid, people, text, logos, franchise insignia or UI.

The initial poster's full concept and art direction are saved in [the implementation plan](../../../docs/ambience-3d-plan.md).
