# Midnight Archive artwork

All environment artwork is original and was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. No official franchise artwork is used.

- `midnight-archive.png`: the original architectural background from the first slice.
- `archive-desk.png` / `archive-desk.webp`: transparent foreground desk, books, and instruments. The WebP is the application asset (268,984 bytes).
- `archive-paper.png` / `archive-paper.webp`: quiet archival-paper texture. The WebP is the application asset (109,242 bytes).

PNG originals are retained alongside the optimized WebP copies. Encoding was optimized with Sharp; the artwork content was preserved. Architectural art and the foreground use responsive Next.js images. Paper is a small shared texture.

## Desk prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent foreground environmental layer for an original immersive web experience named Pensieve, the Midnight Archive. Wide horizontal 16:9 composition.
Primary request: A monumental old dark walnut archive desk seen from the front and slightly above, at seated scholar eye level. The broad empty writing surface fills the central 75 percent. The desk has a thick carved front edge, believable finely worn wood grain and deep brown-indigo shadows. At the far LEFT corner: two modest piles of ancient closed linen-bound books and a small rolled map. At the far RIGHT corner: a small worn brass astronomical instrument and two slender warmly lit candles, with a discreet ink bottle. The objects are restrained, small relative to the broad surface, and stay at the perimeter. Amber candlelight grazes the left and center; faint pale-blue moonlight grazes the right edge. Sophisticated magical realism, cinematic painterly realism with finely observed materials, elegant and mature, original fantasy design.
Composition: complete wide desk silhouette cut out on genuine transparent background; front edge near bottom; empty central writing surface. No room, no wall, no floor, no background, no people, no writing, no UI, no logos or recognizable movie props. Keep every important object inside the canvas. This is a foreground cutout to layer over an existing enormous moonlit library. Real transparency around the desk and objects.

## Paper prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a seamless premium reading-surface texture for the Pensieve Midnight Archive interface, square.
Primary request: A close-up flat sheet of clean warm ivory archival paper, approximately #F0E5CE, with extremely subtle real paper fibers and fine natural grain. Even soft illumination. Delicate tonal variation only. The texture must be comfortable behind long readable ink text, feel expensive, quiet, scholarly and authentic. No strong aging, no stains, no torn edges, no folds, no shadows, no vignette, no writing, no marks, no symbols, no objects, no borders. Entire canvas is paper; seamless consistent surface. Not gritty or distressed.
